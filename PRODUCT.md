# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Budget-conscious football/soccer fans in Latin America and Spain looking for affordable jerseys, team shirts, and sports clothing. They know what they want, they are price-sensitive, and they are wary of buying from Chinese marketplaces: they need to trust that a store is real, reachable, and worth clicking through to.

## Product Purpose

ShirtScanner searches many Chinese sports-clothing providers at the same time from one query, streaming results back as they arrive, and lets the user compare products, prices, and provider health in one place. It exists because searching each marketplace separately is slow and full of dead ends. Success looks like this: a user goes from search to a product they would order in the fewest possible taps, and trusts the store-status signals the whole way.

## Brand Personality

Fast, scrappy, streetwear. Direct and plain-spoken with a bit of match-day swagger. Loud visuals, sober claims. Never corporate, never sketchy, never austere.

## Anti-references

- Corporate / slick SaaS: gradients, glassmorphism, hero-metric templates, enterprise polish
- Sketchy drop-shopper: fake urgency, countdown timers, hype pricing, clipart, bait-and-switch
- Austere magazine minimalism: hushed editorial restraint has no place in a scrappy search tool

## Design Principles

1. **Speed is the product.** One query hitting many stores at once is the whole point. Results stream in as they arrive, progress is always visible, and no screen ever makes the user wait without feedback. The next step must be obvious in one glance.
2. **Pocket-first.** The phone is the primary canvas: most use happens one-handed, mid-transit, outdoors. Every layout assumes a small screen first, thumb reach at bottom, 44px touch targets, no hover-only affordances, and desktop is the enhancement not the baseline.
3. **Scrappy, but not shady.** Rough edges are fine; fakery is not. No urgency timers, no fake discounts, no dark patterns. The honest signal (what we found, what we missed, which stores are up) always wins.
4. **Match-day energy, not hype.** Bold type and color like a stadium poster, but the copy stays specific and truthful. Volume comes from layout and color, never from exaggerated wording.
5. **Show the goods first.** Products and prices are the content. Chrome, prose, and decoration are second. Result density beats ornament.
6. **Trust through transparency.** Users are skeptical of Chinese-marketplace shopping. Surface provider health, result counts, and the "check directly at the store" path prominently, because that is what turns a skeptic into a buyer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast on body copy (3:1 on large text), full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support for every animation.
